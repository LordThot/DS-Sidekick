General Knowledge

Stack Size

  • "Few": Less than 4 units
  • "Handful": 5 to 8
  • "Several": 9 to 21
  • "Pack": 22 to 81
  • "Many": 82 to 128
  • "Gathering": 129 to 227
  • "Horde": 228 to 462
  • "Throng": 463 to 815
  • "Host": 816 to 2,500
  • "Legion": 2,501 to 9,999
  • "Myriad": 10,000 to 25,000
  • "Sea": 25,001 to 49,999
  • "Cornucopia": 50,000 or more

Map speed

  • 1 arrow: 1-7 squares per hour
  • 2 arrows: 8-16 squares per hour
  • 3 arrows: 17-25 squares per hour
  • 4 arrows: 26-41 squares per hour
  • 5 arrows: 41+ squares per hour

Human Statues

Send a spy (no matter which kind, but t2 are faster) to each one of the locations listed below. For each one you will discover the name of a famous spy and get a Scribe of Allembine, which provide a strong defense against spies.

Once you get all 20 locations you will be awarded a discovery (Allembine Research) which will give you up to 100 extra research points per hour (5 per library level)

Some of the locations will give you a clue which will require a follow up visit to a second location; they need to be done in that order or the Scribe of Allembine will not be awarded

A simple way to complete this discovery is to build 20 spies and send them to the listed first locations; after they all come back send to the second locations. You can feed your spies to Audrey after that.

Steamtastic Brewery

Send 125 T2 Spies to the Steamtastic Brewery to be awarded the Gnome Brew discovery

This allows you to use 20% less food to brew beer.

Temple of Reason

You will need you library to be at level 7 or below for this discovery to work.

Send spies to the Temple of Reason and if you are lucky some of them will gain up to 50 research points.
